What this cannot tell you

The limits of the system, and the API key in this folder.
Before you start. PACWATCH flags behaviour that a person should look at. It does not decide what anyone intends, and it is built so that it cannot pretend to. Every detection shows its evidence next to the innocent explanations for the same behaviour. Ask the assistant whether a vessel is hostile and watch what it does — that is the most portable thing in this folder.
Real coastline, depths, tides and port positions. Synthetic vessels and one fictional exercise area, both labelled as such in docs/DATA-DICTIONARY.md. Nothing here is classified, controlled, or operational.
